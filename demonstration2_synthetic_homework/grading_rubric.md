## Partial-credit and interpretation rules

For P6 emissivities (6 points), give 1 point for each correct absorbed-plus-internal power and 2 points for each correct emissivity supported by valid work. The initial six-face balance is assessed separately. For the Earth extension, use a nadir-facing face, uniform Earth disk, and grey thermal absorptivity equal to emissivity; flag different justified conventions for instructor review.

For P5, direct/reflected components and the final total are separate criteria. Preserve correct component credit when the final sum is wrong. A wrong reflected component loses that component credit; an incorrect total also loses the separately assessed final-result credit. Do not additionally penalize correct projected fluxes or the direct actinic component.

Other criteria can receive justified partial credit for valid supporting work. Explain each deduction, avoid repeated penalties outside separately assessed criteria, and do not assign credit to missing calculations. Use S_0 = 1361 W m^-2, SI constants, and spectroscopic rather than angular wavenumber; accept reasonable alternative constants.
